## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** The impact of the identified issues on the feature health
- **Value proposition:** Identify high priority issues to address from the list of identified issues based on their impact to operational health of the feature

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Issues | Are the issues listed accurate? | Yes / No | When even a single issue is inaccurate | n/a | When all the identified issues are real issues |
| Impact on feature health | How well did the agent estimate the impact of the issues on feature health? | 1 to 5 | Not accurate | When impact is either overstated or understated even for a single issue | Completely accurate |
| Prioritization | How well did the agent prioritize the issues? | 1 to 5 | Not accurate | Acceptable with room to improve | Completely accurate |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** All answers for Impact Eval once a day
- **Who scores them with you:** 2 other PMs along with me
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** When we have a disagreement, we will discuss and align on the impact of the issues on feature health.
And then update the agent with additional context.

## Pass bar

An average of 4 or more counts as pass and Yes to the Accuracy question

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Any question that is rated as a 1 will be considered as a fail
