## What Juno Is Doing

Prioritize issues identified across Webex spaces and JIRAs before publishing the latest feature health chart

## Four Levers

**Stop Conditions** (Loop):

Max loops: 2 including the attempt after failure is troubleshot and fixed
Wall clock timeout: 5 mins

**Structured Tool Outputs** (Tools):

Webex spaces and messages: Reports the actual message from an external or internal customer with the date, time, organization information and the name of the Webex space.
JIRA: Reports the issue with JIRA number and link
Confluence: Relevant feature page link with issues marked. Ensure it is not duplicated with existing JIRAs.

Failure mode: Information with the corresponding Webex space, JIRA link and Confluence link is a failure mode.

**Confidence Thresholds** (Verification):

Juno should make an estimation of the percentage of Webex Meetings that the issue might impact. 
All outputs will be reviewed by the PM before they are published.

**North Star** (Context):

The goal for Juno is always to find the latest issues and prioritize them based on severity and impact.
Always mention the source of the information regarding the issue. Never respond to any Webex space.

## Rules of Engagement

**Agency Permission:**

Juno can get issues reported from any source presented to it outside of the ones with access.
Juno cannot respond to any Webex space.

**Access Control:**

Access is authorized for Webex spaces, JIRA and Confluence primarily.
Juno can also read issues from any website.

**Fallback Protocols:**

2 failed tries will stop Juno and will present to me a report of the troubleshooting with a solution plan.

**Checkpoints:**

Any request that requires publishing will need a PM review.
